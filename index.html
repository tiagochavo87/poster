What the poster shows

  • An active-myeloma transcriptional signature is already present in MGUS and SMM plasma cells.
  • Deep learning and CopyKAT aneuploidy calls agree on the same cells (87.4% concordance, κ = 0.70).
  • The malignant program is druggable: WHSC1/MMSET, FGFR3, IL6R, BCMA.
  • Malignant burden varies between patients within the same stage (Kruskal–Wallis p = 0.018).
